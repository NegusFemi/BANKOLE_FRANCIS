    <h2>MEET <br>BANKOLE FRANCIS</h2>
    <p>
      Bankole Francis is a gospel Minister, Recording Artist, Vocal Instructor, Drummer, and Songwriter with a powerful and dynamic vocal range, backed by over 20 years of singing experience. A versatile praise and worship leader, he has shared the stage with both international and Nigerian Gospel artists, passionately using music as a tool to win souls for Jesus Christ.<br /><br />
      Over the years, he has worked with notable artists including Tosin Martins, Gloria Braimah, Esther Osaji, Tope Alabi, Tony Adoki, Timi Dakolo, and Chris Shalom. For 13 years, he served at RCCG Tabernacle of David, Ajah, Lagos as lead vocalist, backup drummer, and band coordinator. In 2022, he ministered as a backup vocalist alongside Tope Alabi at The Experience, one of the world's largest gospel concerts.<br /><br />
      Since relocating to Scotland in 2023, Bankole has continued to minister across the UK. He is currently a music minister at Lovebridge Community Church, Aberdeen, and has been privileged to serve at conferences and concerts including:
    </p>
    <ul>
      <li style="list-style: none;">Peniel Conference - RCCG Jesus House, Aberdeen</li>
      <li style="list-style: none;">African Praise 10th Edition - RCCG Glasgow</li>
      <li style="list-style: none;">Night of Worship - RCCG Jesus House Torry, Aberdeen</li>
    </ul>
    <p>
      Beyond the church, he partners with organisations such as the Mamacita Foundation, bringing encouragement through music at charity events. He is also committed to outreach, ministering in care homes where his music brings joy and comfort to the elderly.<br /><br />
      With a heart for worship and a passion for souls, Bankole Francis continues to raise a sound that glorifies God and inspires transformation across nations.
    </p>
